02 / PHOTO JOURNAL

The gallery.

Eight photographs, eight moods. Each frame has a small story of its own—tap any photograph for the full view and its notes.

A PERSONAL COLLECTION
01—08

MORE THAN A PORTRAIT

Every picture has
something to say.

From an airport selfie and everyday denim to a signature black look and delicate traditional embroidery, this collection captures different sides of Mamtu. The notes below are editorial impressions of the photographs, not invented dates or events.

PHOTO 06 / MONOCHROME / CONFIDENCE

The signature frame

A sleek black look, a mirror, and a pose that feels completely self-assured. This frame is all about quiet confidence and the details that make a simple outfit memorable.

PHOTO 03 / PLAYFUL / PERSPECTIVE

A different angle

A tilted mirror changes the whole mood. The relaxed pose and bold black outfit turn an everyday reflection into a playful little portrait.

PHOTO 01 / TRAVEL / EVERYDAY

Between destinations

Airport lights, a bright red top and a quick selfie before whatever comes next. A more everyday side of the collection, full of movement and possibility.

PHOTO 02 / LITTLE MOMENTS / CANDID

The candid reflection

A white top, a mirror and the bustle of people in the background. It feels like one of those unplanned snapshots worth keeping.

PHOTO 04 / CASUAL / STYLE

Denim & attitude

Black and denim, a side-on pose and a little attitude. This portrait lets an everyday outfit do the talking.

PHOTO 05 / DETAILS / REFLECTION

One more glance

Taken in a similar setting, this second denim portrait shifts the focus to the smaller things: an angle, a bracelet, and a different expression.

PHOTO 07 / COLLECTION / FOUR FRAMES

Four ways to be me

Four photographs come together in one collage. A change of pose and perspective creates four distinct impressions of the same signature look.

PHOTO 08 / TRADITIONAL / DETAIL

A softer side

Lavender embroidery, jewellery and delicate henna details give this portrait a different kind of elegance. A gentle contrast to the bolder black-and-denim frames.

A NOTE ABOUT THE COLLECTION

“A photograph can catch a look. A whole gallery gets a little closer to a personality.”
